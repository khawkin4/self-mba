# Note · LSEG, Microsoft and the AI narrative — from *Workspace inside Teams* to *LSEG Everywhere*: a desktop vendor recasting itself as the licensed data layer for other people's agents, before the money shows up

  **Caveat:** every adoption number (MCP customers, AI Search users) is LSEG-stated. No AI revenue figure is disclosed.
- **Core claim:** The 2022 Microsoft deal was sold as a step-change for cloud and the desktop. It came with $2.8bn of committed spend, £250–300m of extra cost and revenue "from 2025." It delivered slowly, as LSEG and Microsoft both admitted in 2025. Meanwhile the market's question flipped from *will Microsoft help LSEG?* to *will LLMs make LSEG's data and desktop worthless?* Management answered by redefining the company. It became "LSEG Everywhere," an LLM-agnostic licensed-data layer reachable through MCP from Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Databricks and Snowflake. It claims that 90% of Data & Feeds revenue is "non-replicable by an LLM." It also bought back record amounts of stock. Adoption is real and growing fast: 90 → 300+ MCP customers in 2026. But AI revenue "won't move the needle in 2026," core D&A growth is flat at ~5%, and the ASV KPI is being retired.

## 2. Act I — Microsoft as the cloud and desktop answer (2022–2025)

- **The pitch.** "We believe our partnership with Microsoft will transform the way our customers discover, analyse, and trade securities around the world" (Schwimmer, 2022-12-12). "Advances in the cloud and AI will fundamentally transform how financial institutions research, interact, and transact across asset classes" (Satya Nadella, 2022-12-12). Microsoft's Judson Althoff added: "no system can be any more intelligent than the data over which it reasons" (2022-12-12).
- **The cost.** LSEG committed to $2.8bn of minimum cloud spend, £250–300m of incremental cash cost and a 50–100bp margin drag. It took a big shareholder (~4%) bought from the Blackstone/TR consortium, which eased the overhang (see [[lseg-refinitiv-deal]]).
- **The delivery.** First commercial products came in H2 2024 / Dec 2024, and Open Directory slipped to H2 2025. In Nov 2025 Ron Lefferts admitted "this process has not always been as fast as we would have liked." Microsoft's Matt Kerner said "LSEG is bumping into some product gaps and some sharp edges" as the first Copilot Studio MCP connector. Schwimmer reframed the revenue as a "steady upward trajectory as opposed to a spike" (2025-11-10).
- **Exclusivity faded.** By H1 2024 management said there was no exclusive relationship. AWS became preferred cloud for Markets, Risk Intelligence and FTSE Russell (2025-04-28). Real-time feed infrastructure went to AWS (2026-01-15). Dell, Broadcom, Snowflake, Databricks and NVIDIA followed in 2026.

## 3. Act II — "LSEG Everywhere" as the answer to AI disruption (2025–2026)

- **The narrative problem, as management names it.** "the biggest issue by far has been dealing with the perception of the impact of AI versus the reality" (Schwimmer, H1 2026). "We do not agree with it, and all fact-based evidence would indicate the negative market narrative is wrong." (FY25, 2026-02-26)
- **The defence, in three claims:**
  1. *Proprietary data*: "Ninety percent of our revenue is from data that is non-replicable by an LLM." (2025-10-23). On Octus: "No LLM is going to be able to scoop that up anywhere." (2026-10-22)
  2. *AI is a complement, not a substitute*: "So the future is AI integrated into a desktop, not AI replacing a desktop." (2025-07-31). "The value of data has decoupled from the number of people using it." (2026-07-30). "AI has enhanced the value of LSEG." (2026-07-30)
  3. *Openness beats walled gardens*: "So I understand some of our competitors have more of a closed box mentality to this kind of opportunity set." (2026-04-23)
- **Who pays for compute.** "It's our clients who are paying the tokens to the LLMs." (Proch, 2026-04-23). Emily Prince: "LSEG does not run the customer's model inference or control token usage." (2026-07-17). On capex: "we are not in the business of buying chips for billions of dollars." (Proch, 2025-11-10). Irfan Hussain (CIO): "We don't think of ourselves in the model game." (2025-11-10)
- **Monetisation, in management's words.** "MCP, for sure, will be monetized." / "certainly it won't move the needle in 2026." (Proch, 2026-07-30). "this is a marathon, not a sprint, and LSEG is the best running partner." (Schwimmer, 2026-07-30). The Q3 2026 release says "80% of the AI agreements struck with customers thus far involve the purchase of additional datasets."

## 5. What the claims don't prove

1. **The 90% non-replicable claim is a classification LSEG made of its own revenue** (Q3 2025: Data & Feeds split into proprietary real-time, specialised, exclusive, enriched public and pure public). No third party audits the boundary.
2. **Adoption ≠ revenue.** MCP customers and tool calls are counted, but no AI revenue is disclosed. The 80%-of-AI-deals statistic has no denominator in pounds.
3. **The claim that agents consume about 10x the data is flagged by the CEO as "anecdata"** (H1 2026).
4. **Retiring ASV when it slows** removes the main external check on subscription momentum. The replacements (gross sales, retention 92–93%, new-product vitality ~25%) are new and not comparable to history.
5. **Competitor-accuracy attacks** (a rival’s "accuracy in finance is at 51%", Jul 2025) cite rivals' own releases. They are rhetorical, not a controlled test.

## 6. Strategic read (inference)

- **LSEG chose to be a supplier to the AI layer rather than to fight it.** That avoids the closed-box posture it attributes to rivals. It accepts the risk that LLM front-ends capture the customer relationship and turn LSEG into wholesale data. Analysts raised exactly this: Bolzoni asked about "a race to the bottom for data consumption", and Lam said "it would be harder to raise pricing in the future".
- **The real moat is licensing and entitlement plumbing.** That means real-time network reach (575+ venues), usage rights, and audit trails ("If you can’t demonstrate that quality data sources have been used, that is a liability, it’s a risk." — LSEG sales director, 2026-10-06). The bet is that compliance, not model quality, governs bank AI adoption. Schwimmer: "there is a greater sense of conservatism around the usage of AI." (2024-10-24)
- **Microsoft went from cornerstone to one partner among many.** A 10-year, $2.8bn commitment bought an early position. "LSEG Everywhere" is the hedge against that bet. AWS, Google (Gemini), Anthropic and OpenAI all get the same data.
- **Buybacks are the second half of the AI defence.** Management repeatedly ties repurchases to the "dislocation" in the share price (see [[lseg-capital-allocation]]).

## 7. Canon lenses

- **Complements vs substitutes** ([[canon-shapiro-varian-information-rules]], [[canon-hagiu-wright-multi-sided-platforms]]): MCP makes LSEG data a complement to every LLM. The risk is the platform owner commoditising the complement.
- **Disruption** ([[canon-christensen-innovators-dilemma]]): seat-based desktops are the classic incumbent product. LSEG is trying to self-disrupt ("Just to be blunt about it, we're willing to disrupt ourselves." — Schwimmer, 2023-11-17).
- **Paranoia and inflection points** ([[canon-grove-only-the-paranoid-survive]]): the 2025 de-rating as a strategic inflection point that management must name.
- **Narrative vs evidence** ([[canon-kahneman-thinking-fast-and-slow]], [[canon-tetlock-superforecasting]]): both sides argue from anecdote. A falsifiable test would be D&A growth over 6% by 2027 with ASV-equivalent disclosure.

## 8. Practitioner counterpoint

- **For LSEG:** banks will not let models touch unlicensed or unaudited data. Licensed, entitled, low-latency data is exactly what agents need, and agents multiply queries. 80% of AI deals adding datasets is the right leading indicator if true.
- **Against:** seat counts fund D&A today. If agents replace analysts, seat revenue falls before usage revenue rises. Proch's "won't move the needle" plus flat D&A growth means 2025–26 buybacks were paid from Markets and margin, not AI.
