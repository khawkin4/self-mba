## 1 · The "101" crash courses — accessible explainers of the finance machine

- **Private Equity 101.** PE = private ownership + **control** (majority stake) + turnaround ("house flipping"); VC backs
  early/risky, PE buys mature-but-bloated fixer-uppers. Money model = **"2 and 20"** (2% management fee + 20% carried
  interest). Capital stack = LPs + own capital ("skin in the game") + **debt**. **LBO mechanics:** borrow most of the
  price, repay from the target's own cash flow — $100K equity + $900K debt on a $1M asset, add $100K value = 2× on your
  cash. Same leverage *burns the house down* (Toys "R" Us: $5.2B debt → all cash to debt service → couldn't fight Amazon
  → bankrupt, 30K+ jobs). Bad-rep levers: layoffs, short-term profit over health, asset stripping, sensitive industries.
  Counter-case: Blackstone/Hilton ~$14B, no mass layoffs. Dirty secret: most exits = **selling to a bigger PE firm.**
  *(↔ [[canon-jensen-meckling-agency-theory]] — debt as a discipline device and a misalignment lever; maps to Course 02,
  [[unit-09-capital-light-businesses]].)*
- **VC 101.** Staged equity bets (pre-seed → seed → A $3–15M → B/C/D → growth equity); earliest stages have *no revenue*,
  so you bet the **team**. Evaluation = **Team, Market (TAM), Traction.** Inflated TAM works *against* you — the
  convertible slice is what matters. VC is a **signaling/power game** (one marquee fund triggers a pile-on; YC's value is
  partly the signal). **Dark side — structural misalignment:** funds need a few 100–1000× winners and expect most to zero,
  so they push grow-at-all-costs (WeWork, Quibi); a 3–5× outcome great for *you* is a "loser" for *them*. Founders' #1
  regret: taking money too early/at all. *(Reinforces [[harlem-capital-vc-insights]] fund-size-as-master-constraint;
  ↔ [[canon-thiel-zero-to-one]], [[canon-parker-platform-revolution]]; Course 10.)*
- **Personal Finance 101.** Start with **low-cost S&P 500 index funds**, $50–100/mo (index beats actively-managed for
  almost everyone — the Mauboussin "paradox of skill" in practice). **Automate** in priority order: 401(k) to the
  employer match (free money) → HYSA. Emergency fund = **6× monthly expenses**; cash should earn ~4% (HYSA/MMF/CDs);
  high earners in CA/NY/NJ use **Treasuries** (state-tax-exempt). Advanced: max 401(k) then **Mega Backdoor Roth** (+$30–40K/yr
  tax-free). Targets: **work-optional ≈ 30× annual income**; **4% safe withdrawal rate** ($1M → $40K/yr); save **≥15% of
  gross**. Under-discussed: learning to *spend* (cites *Die With Zero*). *(↔ [[canon-mauboussin-success-equation]],
  [[canon-klarman-margin-of-safety]]; maps to [[unit-06-whats-a-business-worth]], Course 02; ↔ [[eyl-insights]] for the
  ownership-first variant.)*
- **How $100M families manage money (Bessemer, ~$200B AUM).** Family office = full-life financial team; real product =
  **peace of mind.** Structural moat = **aligned incentives** (privately owned, no product-pushing = a true fiduciary).
  Start with **"what's the money for?"** (~2/3 of clients are first-gen → goal is passing on *values*, giving heirs
  *choices*). Big-money edge = **long time horizon** → can hold illiquid PE/VC locked 5–10+ yrs (retail can't). DIY:
  be a **"family office of one"** — define the why, auto-split each paycheck (save/spend/donate), review on a calendar
  cadence. "Have it all, but not at the same time" (seasons, not balance). *(↔ [[canon-jensen-meckling-agency-theory]]
  fiduciary/agency; Course 02/11.)*
- **B-school recaps — the non-obvious bits.** **Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz MVPs**: Dropbox video → 75K signups (no product),
  Zappos shipped retail-bought shoes, DoorDash = "Palo Alto Delivery" webpage. Fake the backend, validate demand first
  ("your business plan is a fantasy"). **Match leadership style to company stage**: laissez-faire (tiny) → transactional
  (growth, needs a rubric) → transformational (mature, audacious). **Bezos Type-1/Type-2 decisions** — spend brainpower
  proportional to reversibility. *(↔ [[canon-ries-lean-startup]], [[canon-bezos-shareholder-letters]],
  [[canon-schein-organizational-culture]]; Courses 03/04/09.)*

## 2 · Investing & stock-picking — Imran Khan's framework (flagship; see deep-dive)

- **The ordering is the strategy** (valuation is LAST): understand the business → read & **diff** the primary filings
  (S-1/10-K/10-Q) → ask "why" 10× → cross-verify → identify the **consensus** and test if it's wrong → *then* check
  valuation (it only tells you how much risk is priced in).
- **Never buy because it's cheap** ("anyone can run a P/E — you're not doing anything incremental"); never short just
  because it's expensive. Find the embedded **fear**, then ask if it's true: consensus-and-wrong = the buy.
- **"The market likes to make a fool of the greatest number of people."** Consensus = unpriced risk → be most fearful
  when everyone agrees. But contrarian ≠ right; consensus can stay right for years (Burry was early). → "contrarian with
  the right timing." **Trust is the foundation of value** — multiples expand on predictability; "the moment you break
  trust, things fall apart."
- **AI tooling:** diff filings year-over-year for language drift ("may" → "will"); feed Reddit/Substack comments for
  sentiment-vs-prior-period. **Start small** ("$100 and intimidated? invest $5") to build pattern recognition *and*
  discover your pain tolerance. *(↔ [[canon-klarman-margin-of-safety]], [[canon-mauboussin-success-equation]],
  [[canon-kahneman-thinking-fast-and-slow]] (consensus = availability/herding), [[canon-taleb-black-swan]]; Course 02.)*

## 3 · Wealth-building deal mechanics — de León & Codie Sanchez

- **Rent-as-royalty (de León, flagship deep-dive).** Couldn't buy private Chick-fil-A equity, so he owned the **land**
  and leased it to them — $200K rent on a $2M-sales site ≈ a **10% top-line royalty** ("for every 10 pieces of chicken,
  one was sold on my behalf"). Repeated with Starbucks/CVS/Cane's/Chase. **Control assets with paper, not capital:**
  zero-down option contracts → get land rezoned (ag → residential) to raise value → *then* finance against the new value;
  pay engineers in sweat equity. Starter move for normals: **buy a duplex, rent the other half to cover the mortgage,**
  own it outright in 15 yrs. "Most constraints are self-made." Fear: name it, box it, **leverage** it. Credentials are an
  "information shortcut" (like the Apple brand) that can also dull your scrappiness.
- **Buy a business with $0 (Codie Sanchez).** Three no-cash structures: **seller financing** (~60% of small sales already
  use it; owners are 65+ with no succession plan), **sweat equity**, **expertise-for-equity** — plus LBOs. Target: boring/
  stable SMB, ~$5–30M price, $1–5M profit, 5+ yrs of consistent checks. **Buy a business, not a job.** Diligence rule:
  the **P&L must match the tax return** — if they diverge, buy at the lower number; watch fake add-backs. **Roll-ups** →
  economies of scale + **multiple expansion** (combined professionalized entity trades at a higher multiple than the
  parts). "If information were enough, librarians would be rich" — implementation wins.
- **Startup / "be dumb to succeed" (a16z TXO; 2× YC founder).** "Play the rigged game anyway" — win despite, build allies,
  earn the bully pulpit later. **Run life like a startup:** score each day won/lost on physical/mental/3-tasks; cap at 3
  tasks. **"Go Max Dumb"** — reason from first principles, ask "why" 5×; never midwit. Hire people smarter than you.
  *(↔ [[entrepreneurship-operator-playbook]], [[canon-thiel-zero-to-one]], [[canon-ries-lean-startup]],
  [[canon-doerr-measure-what-matters]] (the daily-score = personal OKRs); Course 10.)*

## 4 · Moats, product & creativity — Evan Spiegel (flagship deep-dive)

- **Software is copyable now ("you can copy almost any piece of software in a couple minutes" — more true with AI), so a
  real moat must be ecosystem, network effects, or owned full-stack hardware.** Snap's specs = own the dev tools,
  rendering engine, OS, optical engine → "can't just copy-paste." Defensibility = time + compounding.
- **Design vs. art:** "design has an objective in a way that art doesn't"; clarity of purpose makes the design "reveal
  itself" (camera-first because the purpose is *fast image-sending*; "alive, not polished" so people feel safe expressing).
- **Creativity is a repeatable process, not lightning:** need-finding → ideate → feedback → iterate → prototype; break
  the routine to generate ideas.
- **Pain tolerance > vision; innovation is "fundamentally misunderstood, otherwise it wouldn't be innovation."** EQ is the
  scarce asset as AI commoditizes IQ. *(↔ [[canon-helmer-7-powers]] (moats), [[canon-cagan-inspired]] +
  [[canon-christensen-innovators-dilemma]] (jobs/need-finding), [[canon-thiel-zero-to-one]] (secrets/contrarian truth);
  Courses 04/05.)*

## 5 · Power, influence & communication (the canon turned into scripts)

- **Power mapping (Pfeffer).** Power = who controls resources/decisions, not title. Sandberg built cross-sector influence;
  Bartz made right moves with no allies → fired. **"Power isn't being right — it's having enough support to survive being
  right."** Exercise: list 5 people who'll influence your next opportunity; classify advocate/neutral/unaware.
- **Meeting tactics.** Be the **summarizer** (speak last, name people → auto-buy-in); **back-channel before the decision
  meeting** so you walk in 85% sure (Goldman commandment #6 — "decisions aren't made in the decision meeting"). Sit *at*
  the table. Reframe nerves as excitement (or do cardio before presenting).
- **Negotiation = expand the pie**, don't split it (the orange: one wants peel, one wants juice). 3-step influence:
  research their goals → frame your want to advance theirs → trade. Trade ranked priority lists *before* talks (Rahm
  Emanuel). **Separate means from ends** (Emanuel on CHIP: give the structural "win," keep the real outcome).
- **Comms frameworks.** **SCQA** (Situation/Complication/Question/Answer — make the point in 30s; Amazon's 6-pager spine);
  **SBI feedback** (Situation/Behavior/Impact; "I felt," never cross the net into intent); **15/5/2** (prep 15-, 5-, 2-min
  versions, default to 2); "**Twitter not Tolstoy**" with senior people. **"Clarity beats intelligence."** Cut hedges
  ("just," "does this make sense?" → "let me know if you have questions"); pause instead of "um." The word **"because"**
  raises compliance ~93% (Langer/Xerox study) even with a weak reason.
- **Body language / visibility.** First impression in ~1/10 s; fix "resting business face" and show your **hands** on
  camera (~3–5 fingers headroom; +33% recall). Visibility is engineered: broadcast wins with **"we"** framing, repeat
  ~7× in 7 ways. Promotions are a **months-ahead** strategy game, not a review-day ask. *(↔ [[canon-pfeffer-power]],
  [[canon-greene-48-laws-of-power]], [[canon-cialdini-influence]] + [[canon-cialdini-pre-suasion]] (the "because" effect),
  [[canon-carnegie-win-friends]], [[canon-fisher-ury-getting-to-yes]] + [[canon-voss-never-split-the-difference]]
  (expand-the-pie/mirroring), [[canon-minto-pyramid-principle]] (SCQA), [[canon-scott-radical-candor]] (SBI/feedback),
  [[canon-duarte-resonate]]; Courses 07/08/09.)*
- **"Just Ask" (ex-Match CEO).** A trainable muscle — even a failed ask plants the seed; "squeaky wheels get paid more."
  Pay gets "out of whack" because external hires negotiate up and internal loyalty doesn't. Run pay-equity audits.

## 7 · Career reinvention, confidence & mindset

- **Reinvention frameworks (recurring):** Bridges' Transitions (Ending → **Neutral Zone** → New Beginning — don't skip the
  middle); Dweck growth mindset (a *mode*, not an identity); Burnett/Evans *Designing Your Life* (**prototype cheaply**
  before committing — one host invented a 1-day/week unpaid internship to test a 20-yr-old hypothesis); Atomic Habits;
  Brené Brown vulnerability; Esther Perel narrative-rewrite.
- **The 12-month projection test:** "On this exact path, would I be happy in 12 months?" Rate career/relationship/identity
  1–10; **under 7 = yellow flag.** Watch for **"value drift"** and the **zone-of-excellence trap** (great at something
  shiny but unfulfilled — the quietest trap because external validation makes it hard to leave).
- **Toxic boss:** HR serves the company, not you. Document everything, build cross-functional visibility, exit. Vet your
  *next* boss: pick one whose former reports **followed them across companies.**
- **Confidence (Jim Keyes, ex-7-Eleven/Blockbuster):** "Prepare to win" — confidence is reps, not nature. Three C's:
  Change → Confidence → Clarity. (Sharp aside: Blockbuster failed on **cash/balance-sheet management**, not tech — it
  belongs in the accounting course.)
- **Childhood patterns (Jane Chen):** achievement is often a trauma response; "you can't talk your way out of trauma, you
  feel through it" (IFS "parts work," *No Bad Parts*). The **"whole-body yes"** decision filter (head + heart + gut align).
- **Overthinking (Jeremy Liew):** skip fake prerequisites; **"slipstream"** behind people you admire; getting fired for
  *character* was his most-grateful failure. "Strong opinions loosely held" + "disagree and commit."
- **Rejection (David Shim):** "failure training" — every objection becomes your **feature roadmap**; you'll be forgotten,
  so re-pitch the same person. Competition educates the market *for* you.
- **Grinding is overrated (Jonah Goodhart):** "find your pause" (daily/weekly/sabbatical); **"active rest vs. distracted
  rest"**; happiness-quotient over regimen. *(↔ [[canon-clear-atomic-habits]], [[canon-heath-switch]] (change),
  [[canon-horowitz-what-you-do-is-who-you-are]] (character), [[canon-walsh-score-takes-care-of-itself]] (process over
  outcome), [[canon-newport-deep-work]] (pause/focus); Course 09, [[unit-01-reading-the-scoreboard]] mindset.)*

## 8 · Dating, relationships & relationship-as-strategy

- **Recurring dating frameworks:** Happiness = **Stability + Juice + X-Factor** (don't over-index on what your last
  relationship lacked); the **stack-rank values exercise** (compare top life priorities — comfort with *their* ranking,
  not identical rankings); Gottmans' **Eight Dates**; **"Run your love life like a CEO"** (choose don't prove; emotional
  due diligence; **patterns over potential**; define deal terms; emotional equity).
- **Take people at face value** — who they *are* = what they *do*, not what they say ("stop being Barbara the Builder").
  **"Hire slow, fire fast"** applies to relationships — they always wished they'd ended things sooner; trust the gut/
  intuition. Freeze **eggs not embryos** (an ex can legally block embryos; tech narrowed the gap to ~4%).
- **Marriage as a business deal (Maggie Sellers):** "Everyone has a prenup — you write it or the government does"; spend
  more time on the prenup than the wedding; couples therapy *before* problems (written agenda, monthly). Spouse = **life
  co-founder**; treat with contracts, lanes, and repair rituals. "Ostriching is expensive" (avoidance multiplies cost ~10×).
- **Should you marry for money:** don't marry *for* it, but it's the **#1 financial decision** — use someone's relationship
  to money as a signal of drive/compatibility. **Always keep your own money/career/autonomy** (money becomes control).
- **Relationship recession (econ analogy):** ~50% of 25–34 men / 41% women partnerless; confidence crisis + emotional-capital
  hoarding + situationships = "micro-dosing vulnerability." Levers: normalize low-stakes honesty, build community,
  de-center romance. **Male loneliness** is a *public* problem because men metabolize it as **anger**; "social snacking,"
  the monetized "manosphere," male body-dysmorphia.
- **"Let Them" (Mel Robbins) critique:** breaks down without **"Let Me"** (fill the freed space) + an action item (state
  the need first); fails under power imbalance, conflict-avoidance, accountability vacuum. Use an **"Ice Box" notes folder**
  instead of reacting. *(↔ [[canon-fisher-ury-getting-to-yes]] (interests, BATNA in relationships),
  [[canon-mauboussin-success-equation]] (signal vs. noise / "zoom out like a scientist"); life-application layer, no course.)*

## 9 · Greatness, brand & life design

- **Greatness (Rabil bros, PLL):** strength + speed are common; **endurance** (authentic passion) is the rare
  differentiator. Sports is **AI-defensible** because it *needs* human error ("every brand is trying to elicit a feeling").
  **"Condition your fans"** (the NFL tailgate was manufactured). Define co-founder lanes — "you need friction to run fast."
- **Disney Playbook (Dude Perfect CEO):** content → audience → **experiences** (the flywheel); separate creative from
  analytics (set parameters, not mandates); **focus on a niche** (the family) over mass appeal; "our founders are our
  brand is our culture" → repeat values relentlessly as you scale. *(↔ [[canon-helmer-7-powers]] (brand/durability),
  [[canon-parker-platform-revolution]] (flywheel), [[canon-schein-organizational-culture]] +
  [[canon-horowitz-what-you-do-is-who-you-are]] (scaling values); Courses 04/05.)*
- **Life design (2026 rules / life-is-short / success-isn't-linear):** use the **"fresh-start effect"** + neutral zone
  (Jan 1 → Lunar New Year as a 6-week runway). Tools laddered simple→complex: word-of-the-year → bingo card → "X things
  while I'm X" (over-provision to ~70 so you "win") → vision board → scripting. **Happiness = reality − expectations.**
  Compounding applies to *relationships* (Imran: a "real" friend = active texting, reply within a minute). **Emotional
  evenness** as a power source; **learn from younger people** as you age. First job's superpower = proof you can learn
  anything. *(↔ [[canon-clear-atomic-habits]], [[canon-doerr-measure-what-matters]] (goals→key results).)*
