# Note · Hagiu & Wright — *Multi-Sided Platforms*

> Canon · Cluster: Network Effects & Platform Strategy · Maps to **Course 04** (Competitive Strategy), **Course 03** (Micro & Strategy Foundations)

- **Source:** Andrei Hagiu & Julian Wright, *Multi-Sided Platforms* (research synthesis, 2015)
- **Core claim:** The economics of **why platforms subsidize one side** — pricing both sides
  symmetrically is the classic strategic error.
- **Framework:**
  - **Subsidize the side that is (a) more price-sensitive, or (b) generates more cross-side network
    effects.** Charge the other side.
    - Google: **free search to users**, charge advertisers.
    - Payment networks: **subsidize cardholders**, charge merchants.
    - Adobe (historically): free reader, paid creator.
  - **Indirect (cross-side) network effects** — the value a user on one side gets from users on the
    *other* side — determines optimal pricing *and* investment priority. Measuring it is the core
    analytical challenge.
  - A "platform" is a real platform only if **both sides must be affiliated and cross-side effects
    are strong**; otherwise it's just a reseller/input supplier.
- **Applies when:** setting pricing and go-to-market for any two-sided business — deciding which side
  pays and which you subsidize to ignite the flywheel.
- **Breaks when:** the elegant pricing logic assumes you can **measure indirect network effects** —
  in practice they're hard to estimate, so "subsidize the right side" is clearer in theory than
  execution. Over-subsidizing the wrong side (or both) is exactly how marketplaces **burn capital
  with no flywheel** (the WeWork/many-a-startup failure mode). And a heavily subsidized side can be
  **disloyal/multi-homing**, never converting to the value you priced for.
- **Practitioner counterpoint:** (fill via `research` → r/startups, r/venturecapital) — the operative
  question founders ask: *which side is the "money side" and which is the "subsidy side,"* and is the
  cross-side pull strong enough to justify the burn? The micro foundation under
  [[canon-cusumano-business-of-platforms]] and [[canon-parker-platform-revolution]].
