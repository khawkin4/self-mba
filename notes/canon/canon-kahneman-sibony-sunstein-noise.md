# Note · Kahneman, Sibony & Sunstein — *Noise*

> Canon · Cluster: Behavioral Design & Decision Science · Maps to **Course 07** (Data & Decision-Making), **E6** (Crisis Leadership)

- **Source:** Daniel Kahneman, Olivier Sibony & Cass Sunstein, *Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment* (2021)
- **Core claim:** **Bias's overlooked twin is noise.** Bias is *systematic* error (everyone skews the
  same way); **noise is random scatter** (different people — or the same person on different days —
  reach wildly different conclusions from identical facts). Orgs obsess over bias and **almost never
  measure noise**, though noise may do more damage.
- **Framework:**
  - **Total error² = bias² + noise²** — they add; reducing only bias leaves half the problem.
  - Evidence: judges give very different sentences for identical cases; underwriters quote very
    different premiums for the same risk; doctors diagnose the same symptoms differently.
  - **Decision hygiene** to cut noise: gather **independent judgments before discussion** (avoid
    anchoring/cascades), use **shared scales / reference classes**, **aggregate** multiple
    assessments, structure judgments into independent components, and favor rules/algorithms for
    repeated judgments.
- **Applies when:** any organization makes **repeated professional judgments** — hiring, pricing,
  grading, underwriting, performance review — where consistency itself has value.
- **Breaks when:** noise reduction can over-rotate into **rigid algorithmization** that strips
  legitimate, expert, case-sensitive discretion (Klein's recognition-primed expertise is *not*
  noise, [[canon-klein-sources-of-power]] — telling them apart is hard). "Independent judgments
  first" is procedurally costly and culturally resisted. And some variance is signal, not noise —
  flattening it can erase real differences.
- **Practitioner counterpoint:** (fill via `research` → r/datascience, r/managers) — the actionable
  kernel: in hiring/review, **have people score independently *before* the meeting** — the loudest
  voice otherwise sets the anchor. Pairs with Tetlock's calibration ([[canon-tetlock-superforecasting]]).

### Rep
Run a noise audit on one repeated decision: have 3 people independently score the *same* case
without conferring. The spread is your noise. Then add one hygiene step (independent-first) and
re-measure.
