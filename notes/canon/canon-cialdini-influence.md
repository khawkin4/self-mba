# Note · Cialdini — *Influence*

> Canon · Cluster: Negotiation & Influence · Maps to **Course 08** (Negotiation), **Course 05** (Marketing & Brand)

- **Source:** Robert Cialdini, *Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion* (1984)
- **Core claim:** Persuasion runs on a handful of automatic **"click, whirr" triggers**. The value
  isn't learning to manipulate — it's **recognizing the levers being pulled on you.**
- **Framework — the six (later seven) principles:**
  1. **Reciprocity** — people feel obligated to return favors.
  2. **Commitment & consistency** — a small first step → continued commitment in that direction.
  3. **Social proof** — under uncertainty, people copy what others do.
  4. **Authority** — people defer to perceived expertise (titles, uniforms, credentials).
  5. **Liking** — we say yes to people we like (similarity, compliments, cooperation).
  6. **Scarcity** — we want what's disappearing or restricted.
  7. **Unity** (added later) — shared identity ("one of us").
- **Applies when:** designing or defending against persuasion — marketing, pricing, fundraising,
  and spotting when a counterpart is engineering your "yes."
- **Breaks when:** the triggers are **probabilistic, not deterministic** — they shift base rates,
  they don't compel; over-relying on one (manufactured scarcity, fake social proof) breeds backlash
  once detected. Used cynically they corrode trust and brand (the opposite of persuasion long-term).
  And in low-emotion, high-deliberation decisions (System 2,
  [[canon-kahneman-thinking-fast-and-slow]]) the levers weaken. This is applied "psychology of
  misjudgment" — Munger's list overlaps heavily ([[canon-munger-poor-charlies-almanack]]).
- **Practitioner counterpoint:** *(researched 2026-06-05 — r/sales + r/negotiation corpus + Cialdini transcripts)*
  The workhorses show up exactly where the book predicts. A r/sales "**Got ghosted for 97 days. Closed
  $1.3M with a handwritten note**" thread (4277↑) is **reciprocity + scarcity** in the wild — a paper
  note FedEx'd "signed with a blue pen like a psychopath," framing the deal around the CFO's own board
  meeting deadline, pulled a stalled 7-figure deal back to life. **But the recurring pain point is that
  the levers backfire the instant they read as *technique*:** the most-upvoted cautionary thread,
  "**VP made me sit through 6 hours of 'consultative selling' training. Client hung up on me using their
  exact script**" (1017↑) — *"Dude literally laughed and said 'Are you reading from a script?' then hung
  up"* — while the rep who skipped the training "closed two deals this week just talking to people like a
  normal human being." The ethical line Cialdini himself draws holds: principles work only "employing
  them **in an ethical manner**" — the favor, the proof, the scarcity must be *real*, or detection
  poisons everything.
- **Researched layer** *(2026-06-05)*
  - **Canonical explanation (sourced):** the principles are **decision shortcuts**, not arguments —
    *"in the increasingly overloaded lives we lead more than ever we need shortcuts or rules of thumb
    to guide our decision-making… just six of these shortcuts as universals that guide human behavior:
    reciprocity, scarcity, authority, consistency, liking and consensus"* (Cialdini, "Science of
    Persuasion," BigSpeak Speakers Bureau). The lever fires *automatically* under cognitive load — which
    is exactly why it weakens under System-2 deliberation ([[canon-kahneman-thinking-fast-and-slow]]).
  - **The mechanism, verified across transcripts:** **Reciprocity** is *"to be the first to give and to
    ensure that what you give is personalized and unexpected"* — the restaurant-mint study, where one
    mint lifts tips ~3%, two mints ~14%, but a waiter who turns back and adds *"for you nice people,
    here's an extra mint"* drives a **23% increase, "influenced not by what was given but how it was
    given"** (BigSpeak). **Liking** is supercharged by *similarity + genuine compliments*; **scarcity**
    by what is *"limited or dwindling… unique, something they can't get anywhere else"* (Roger Dooley,
    "Brainfluence Brief").
  - **Real application (mined):** the r/sales handwritten-note close (4277↑) stacks three levers at once
    — **reciprocity** (an unexpected, personalized physical gift of effort), **scarcity** (a real,
    self-imposed board-meeting deadline), and **authority/social proof** (the three blockers quoted
    "from your team's words, not mine"). The EA called back the next morning; the 7-minute call revived
    a $1.3M deal that 97 days of "polite bumps" couldn't.
  - **Where the corpus pushes back on the book:** the consultative-selling thread (1017↑) is the live
    failure mode — a *recognized* persuasion script ("Are you reading from a script?") doesn't just
    underperform, it **actively repels**, while the untrained rep wins by sounding human. Practitioners'
    verdict matches Cialdini's own caveat: the principles only hold when the favor/proof/scarcity is
    genuine; weaponized as a rehearsed routine they trip the BS-detector and burn trust.
