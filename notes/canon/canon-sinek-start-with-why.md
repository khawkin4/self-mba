# Note · Sinek — *Start with Why*

> Canon · Cluster: Communication & Storytelling · Maps to **Course 05** (Marketing & Brand), **E7** (Exec Presence & Comms)

- **Source:** Simon Sinek, *Start with Why* (2009)
- **Core claim:** **People don't buy *what* you do — they buy *why* you do it.** Inspiring leaders and
  brands communicate from the inside out, leading with purpose.
- **Framework — the Golden Circle:**
  - **Why** (purpose/belief) at the center → **How** (your differentiating process) → **What**
    (products/services) on the outside.
  - **Most organizations communicate outside-in** (here's what we make, here's how, …, and never get
    to why). **Inspiring ones start at the center** — lead with the belief.
  - **Biological hook (as Sinek frames it):** "why" speaks to the **limbic brain** (emotion,
    decision); "what" speaks to the **neocortex** (analysis). People **decide emotionally and justify
    rationally** — so lead with the emotion.
- **Applies when:** brand positioning, mission/vision communication, recruiting, rallying a team — any
  message whose job is to **inspire belonging or belief**, not to transact information.
- **Breaks when:** it's a **powerful frame riding on shaky science** — the "limbic vs. neocortex"
  neurology is pop-simplified, and the marquee Apple/Wright-Brothers examples are **survivorship-
  picked** (plenty of purpose-led companies failed; plenty of "what-led" ones won). "Why" can become
  **vague inspirational wallpaper** that substitutes for a real product or strategy — purpose doesn't
  pay if the *what* is weak. For a *decision* memo, Minto's answer-first beats a "why" monologue
  ([[canon-minto-pyramid-principle]]).
- **Practitioner counterpoint:** (fill via `research` → r/marketing, r/startups) — marketers keep the
  **lead-with-purpose** instinct for brand/recruiting while rolling their eyes at "why-washing." The
  emotion-first lesson rhymes with Heath's Elephant ([[canon-heath-switch]]) and Kahneman's
  decide-then-justify ([[canon-kahneman-thinking-fast-and-slow]]).
