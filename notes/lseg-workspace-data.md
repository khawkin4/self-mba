# Note · LSEG Workspace and Data & Analytics vs the Bloomberg Terminal — five division heads, a ~30% price discount, the Eikon sunset done, and Workflows still growing ~3%

> Competitive-strategy / product note · Maps to **Course 04** (Competitive Strategy — counter-positioning, switching costs, the #2 player's playbook), **Course 05** (Marketing & Brand — pricing, discount strategy, bundling into enterprise deals), **E5** (Transformation — a 350k-user forced migration), **E1** (Leading at Scale — leadership churn in the biggest division), **Course 07** (KPIs — ASV and its retirement) · Written 2026-10-25 · Part of [[lseg-hub]]; companions [[lseg-ai-everywhere]], [[lseg-refinitiv-deal]]; compare [[bloomberg-hub]], [[bloomberg-terminal-instrumentation]]

- **Source:** LSEG corpus: transcripts Jul 2021 → Oct 2026, especially the investor education day (2021-07-02), the CMD (2023-11-16/17), the Innovation Forum D&A sessions (2025-11-10) and every results call; results releases; ARs 2019–2025; the customer releases (HSBC case in AR 2023, Barclays 2023-05-03, Dow Jones 2024-07-02, UBS 2025-07-22, Citi 2025-12-16, BofA 2026-02-17). **Caveat:** Bloomberg never speaks in this corpus. All comparisons with it are LSEG's or analysts'.
- **Core claim:** LSEG inherited a shrinking desktop. Eikon Premium was down ~6%/yr and professional terminals fell from 174k to 161k (2018–2020). It also inherited a big data-feeds business. Its answer was to price Workspace well below Bloomberg ("about 25% below the cost of competitive products"), sell it in enterprise bundles (LSEG Data Access agreements, LDAs), migrate ~350,000 Eikon users by June 2025, and pitch openness ("not a closed box"). It won real displacements (HSBC 750+ terminals) and signed £1.9bn of long-term bank deals in Q4 2025. But Workflows (the desktop line) still grows about 3% while Data & Feeds grows 7%+. The division had five heads in five years. Its headline KPI (ASV) was retired from 2027 after slowing. The desktop is now a defended position, not a growth engine. LSEG's growth in data comes from feeds, pricing and reference data, where it says it moved from #6 to #3 behind ICE and Bloomberg.

## 2. What LSEG inherited — the candid 2021 diagnosis

- "This business has had a growth challenge. Revenues have declined in recent years" (Dean Berry, Trading & Banking, 2021-07-02). "Eikon premium is our one size fits all desktop offering" (Berry, 2021-07-02).
- Stone's counter-positioning against Bloomberg: "Workspace is not a closed box, one size fits all terminal." and "the per user commercial model is becoming pretty anachronistic in this industry" (2021-07-02). The customer line Stone quoted: "Humans shouldn't be looking at terminals". *(Second-hand, as Stone reported it.)*
- Schwimmer in Oct 2021, when an analyst remarked that Workspace came up more than expected: still "a distribution channel."

| **Bundle into enterprise deals** | LDAs ~17–18% of D&A revenue/ASV (2025–26). HSBC, Barclays, UBS, Citi, BofA, Standard Chartered | Banks use LSEG to consolidate vendors. LSEG trades price per seat for share of wallet |

## 5. What the claims don't prove

1. **The 25–30% price gap is LSEG's own estimate** of a list-price comparison. Bloomberg's real discounts are not disclosed.
2. **Displacement stories are hand-picked** (HSBC, a won-back Asian bank, UniCredit "lining up to cancel" then stayed). No share-of-seats data appears in the corpus.
3. **ASV's acceleration in 2023 was largely price** (~3%+ yield, Q1 2023 call). Its fall in 2024–25 was blamed on one-offs (Credit Suisse/UBS ~100bp, Eikon clean-up, "competitor response"). Then the KPI was dropped.
4. **The #3-in-pricing-and-reference-data claim** comes from an analyst's question (Quelch, Q3 2025), not a third-party ranking.

## 6. Strategic read (inference)

- **The desktop war is being fought for retention, not share.** At ~3% growth with ~3.5% price, Workflows volume is flat to down. That is consistent with a defended #2 that loses seats to automation and gains a few banks per year.
- **LSEG's real data strength is the plumbing.** Real-time feeds to 575+ venues, Tick History, pricing and reference data. "In financial markets, data has become infrastructure." (Schwimmer, FY25). That business is less exposed to seat decline and more exposed to AI demand.
- **Leadership churn is the tell.** Craig → Stone → Schwimmer (interim) → Singh → Lefferts/Biagini, in five years. Four of the five came from rivals or other industries (Bloomberg, Mastercard, S&P). Schwimmer calls the turnover "feature, not a bug" (Nov 2025). Readers may disagree.

## 7. Canon lenses

- **Counter-positioning** ([[canon-helmer-7-powers]]): an open, bundled, cheaper workstation is hard for Bloomberg to copy without cannibalising per-seat pricing. But Bloomberg's network effect (IB chat) is the stronger power.
- **#2 strategy and price signalling** ([[canon-porter-competitive-strategy]], [[canon-dixit-nalebuff-thinking-strategically]]): "we're never going to take the price up 29%" signals that LSEG will not close the gap by price. That invites Bloomberg not to cut.
- **Switching costs** ([[canon-shapiro-varian-information-rules]]): the Eikon sunset deliberately reset LSEG's own customers' switching costs. Every renewal became contestable.
- **Platforms** ([[canon-cusumano-business-of-platforms]]): Workspace inside Teams/Excel is a complementor strategy on Microsoft's platform.

## 8. Practitioner counterpoint

- **For:** banks are actively consolidating vendors (Barclays: "advance our goals of consolidating relationships with a smaller number of global strategic market data vendors", 2023-05-03). A cheaper, open #2 with real-time plumbing is the natural second vendor.
- **Against:** traders keep Bloomberg for chat and liquidity discovery. Schwimmer admits the voice-trading problem is "people chatting on Bloomberg and then executing" (2025-11-10). Until Workspace replaces that network, it stays the second screen.
