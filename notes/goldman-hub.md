# Note · Goldman Sachs hub — a trading house that tried to become a consumer bank, retreated, and found its "durable" franchise in the balance sheet it already had

> Hub / company-study note · Maps to **Course 04** (Competitive Strategy), **Course 02** (Corporate Finance & Valuation), **Course 09** (Leadership & OB), **E2** (Capital Allocation), **E3** (M&A), **E5** (Transformation & Change), **E8** (Stakeholder & Investor Relations), **E10** (Digital & AI Transformation), **E12** (Culture as Strategy) · Written 2026-10-25 · Entry point for the ten Goldman Sachs notes; the dealer-side companion to [[tradeweb-hub]]

- **Source:** goldmansachs.com as of 2026-10-25, through its 6,996-URL sitemap. I screened all **458 pressroom items for 2019–2026**, **1,316 Insights/Exchanges/Top of Mind pages** and **142 IR pages**, and kept **262 lens-relevant items (~512k words)** (`_ingest/raw/goldman/articles.jsonl`, index `index.tsv`, method and filter in `DIGEST.md`, reader notes in `reader-notes/`). The kept set:
  - 110 press releases, including 32 earnings releases with their PDFs
  - 7 shareholder letters (FY2019–FY2025)
  - Investor Day decks (2020 and 2023)
  - 44 *Exchanges* episodes, 38 with transcripts
  - 24 Insights articles and 5 Top of Mind reports

  **Missing:**
  - **Earnings-call Q&A:** transcripts are available only by request form, which I didn't submit.
  - **Conference webcasts:** audio only.
  - **10-K detail.**
  - **Any venue named besides Tradeweb.**

  Everything here is Goldman's own channel, with internal hosts.
- **Core claim:** The 2020 Investor Day promised higher, steadier returns from four new businesses: Transaction Banking, a digital consumer bank, third-party alternatives and wealth. Six years later the ROE target is met (15.0% in 2025, ~20% in 2026) but by a different route.
  - **Consumer was unwound** in five dated steps: Marcus loans, GreenSky, PFM, the GM card, then Apple Card to Chase (Jan 2026).
  - **Durability came from two places:** the old dealer's balance sheet (financing reached 37% of FICC+Equities revenue in 2025; equities financing passed equities intermediation in Q3 2026) and **AWM fees** (>$11.5bn), bought partly through a string of wrapper and distribution acquisitions.
  - **Around that core sit:**
    - a culture fix (One Goldman Sachs, from 'Balkanized' in 2019 to wallet-share KPIs);
    - a dealer-as-platform strategy (Marquee, Sigma X, ETF Accelerator);
    - a tokenization rail it chose to give away (GS DAP → industry-owned, with Tradeweb);
    - an AI operating model (OneGS 3.0) that Solomon himself calls narrower than its label;
    - a private-credit position on both sides of the market, now under stress.

  The firm narrates all of this through targets and KPIs, which is new for an ex-partnership. Its admissions are short.

## 3. Six patterns across the notes

1. **Same goal, new vehicle.** Every deal and reorganization from 2019 to 2026 is justified as making revenue "more durable": Folio, GreenSky, NN IP, Industry Ventures, Innovator and NEOS. When consumer failed, the vehicle switched to AWM fees and financing; the goal didn't. ([[goldman-strategy-financials]], [[goldman-awm-private-credit]])
2. **The dealer's moat moved from flow to balance sheet.** Electronic execution was ~99.9% of US cash-equity tickets by 2019 (ID20), and the letters stop mentioning electronic share after 2020. Prime, repo and structured lending carry the growth. That is the strategic counterpart to Tradeweb's thesis that automation and data are the product: venues compress spreads, so dealers monetize balance sheet. *(Inference.)* ([[goldman-markets-platforms]], [[tradeweb-hub]])
3. **Mutualize what needs a network, own what needs a balance sheet.**
   - GS spins out the ledger (GS DAP → industry-owned), backs the consolidated tape and co-signs CCP reform.
   - It keeps principal liquidity, financing and private credit in-house.
   - It is a co-investor in the rails venues use (Digital Asset), a launch dealer on them (SOFR swaption on TW SEF) and an adviser when they consolidate (Trumid).

   *(Inference.)* ([[goldman-digital-assets]])
4. **Narrative management through targets and boilerplate.**
   - Public KPIs, PSU-only pay and Investor Day scorecards make the firm accountable.
   - The goalposts move quietly: alts went from net to gross; the top-client metric from top-100 to top-150; returns became "through the cycle".
   - Admissions are one sentence long, and the boilerplate edits ("and consumer banking", in then out) date the strategy more precisely than the letters do. ([[goldman-mission-forces]], [[goldman-consumer-retreat]])
5. **House debates as a product.** GS Research stages its own skeptics: Covello on AI ROI, and Roubini and Eichengreen on crypto and stablecoins. Meanwhile GBM earns fees from the AI capex boom and AWM lends to software. The firm sells insight on both sides of trends it is long. ([[goldman-ai]], [[goldman-outside-voices]])
6. **One CEO voice, a bench of triumvirates.** Solomon speaks for the firm in every release, letter and podcast. Below him: Waldron (operator, Board member since 2025), triumvirate co-heads in IB, FICC and Equities, AWM leaders on the Management Committee (2026), and partners hired from Big Tech into AI and engineering. ([[goldman-people]])

## 4. Cross-note open questions

1. **Earnings-call Q&A:** what do analysts push on (Platform Solutions losses, SCB, private-credit marks), and how do the answers differ from the letters? Source the transcripts elsewhere.
2. **10-K checks:** segment revenue and assets, financing revenue by product, Marquee and electronic revenue (if disclosed), the Tradeweb stake history, and any Trumid or Digital Asset holdings. ([[sec-filing-intelligence]])
3. **Private credit, 2027:** do GS's *technical, not fundamental* readings hold as BDC outflows persist? Watch AWM private-credit marks and Capital Solutions origination volumes.
4. **OneGS 3.0 delivery:** headcount, the efficiency ratio and the six processes (KYC/onboarding first). Does a programme Solomon calls "narrower than that grand vision" turn into measurable savings?
5. **Venue consolidation:** with ICE–MarketAxess and Tradeweb–Trumid closing in H1 2027, does dealer bargaining power over credit venues fall? Does GS respond via principal liquidity, portfolio trading or its own pools?
6. **GS DAP:** does the spin-out close, who joins beyond Tradeweb and BNY, and does it interoperate with Canton?
