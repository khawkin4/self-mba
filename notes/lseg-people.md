# Note · LSEG's people — one CEO and one Chair for seven years, a revolving door everywhere else: Schwimmer's market-structure voice stayed constant while the data business went through five heads and the CFO seat moved from integrator to cost-cutter

> People & perspectives study · Maps to **E1** (Leading at Scale — CEO tenure vs divisional churn), **E7** (Executive Presence & Comms — how Schwimmer, Manz and Proch talk to analysts), **E8** (Stakeholder & IR — the call as a performance), **Course 09** (Leadership & Org Behavior — outside hires from rivals), **E12** (Culture as Strategy — "fixing the basics", "willing to disrupt ourselves") · Written 2026-10-25 · Part of [[lseg-hub]]; companions [[lseg-outside-voices]], [[lseg-mission-forces]]

- **Source:** LSEG corpus: every leadership-appointment and directorate release 2019–2026, the 42 transcripts (Jul 2021 → Oct 2026, where executives speak unscripted), AGM Chair statements, and AR bios and letters. **Caveat:** the press-release quotes are scripted. The transcripts carry the real voice, but they start only in Jul 2021. Pronouns are not stated in the sources, so this note uses names and roles throughout.
- **Core claim:** LSEG is run by a stable pair. **David Schwimmer** (CEO since Aug 2018, ex-Goldman global head of market structure) and **Don Robert** (Chair since 2019, ex-Experian) took LSEG from a European exchange group to a data company. Around them, nearly every other seat turned over, often to hires from rivals:
  - **Data & Analytics heads:** David Craig → Andrea Remyn Stone (ex-Bloomberg) → Schwimmer (interim) → Satvinder Singh (ex-Mastercard) → Ron Lefferts and Gianluca Biagini (ex-S&P).
  - **CFOs:** David Warren → Anna Manz → Michel-Alain Proch.
  - **CIOs:** Chris Corrado → Tony McCarthy → Irfan Hussain (ex-Goldman).
  - **FTSE Russell CEOs:** Waqas Samad → Arne Staal → Fiona Bassett.

  Schwimmer's message held for seven years: open access, multi-asset, the whole trade lifecycle, customer partnership. What changed was the *enemy*, which went from HKEX and integration risk (2019–22) to Bloomberg's walled garden (2023–24) to the AI-disruption narrative (2025–26). The CFO voice changed too: from Manz's candid "fixing the basics" to Proch's "Now it's time to execute."

## 2. Profiles

### David Schwimmer — CEO since 2018
- **Background (AR bios):** 20 years at Goldman Sachs, including global head of market structure.
- **Signature views:** breadth, open access, data and execution as one flywheel, and customer partnership.
  - "liquidity begets liquidity. Data begets data." (2022-03-03)
  - "None of our competitors has the breadth of offering or the global reach of LSEG." (2023-11-16)
  - "We are both a provider of and a consumer of data." (2024-02-29)
- **Voice on calls:** plain and occasionally blunt. "Just to be blunt about it, we're willing to disrupt ourselves." (2023-11-17). Schwimmer rebuts the market directly: "We do not agree with it, and all fact-based evidence would indicate the negative market narrative is wrong." (2026-02-26)
- **Where the view shifted:**
  - 2019: "transform" via Refinitiv.
  - 2023: "2023 is the year that we shift from integration to transformation"
  - 2025–26: AI is a complement: "So the future is AI integrated into a desktop, not AI replacing a desktop." (2025-07-31); "AI has enhanced the value of LSEG." (2026-07-30)
  - Oct 2026: back to M&A with Octus: "we are becoming a global leader in fixed income data and trading." (2026-10-22)
- **On the churn around the CEO:** the frequent senior turnover is a "feature, not a bug" (Nov 2025).

### Don Robert — Chair since 2019
- **2019, combative:** HKEX's bid ran into "fundamental concerns about the key aspects of the Conditional Proposal: strategy, deliverability, form of consideration and value."
- **2021–22, steward:** "LSEG holds a privileged position at the heart of financial markets" (AGM 2021). "We recognise the importance of meeting your expectations and delivering long-term shareholder value." (AGM 2022)
- **2024, register-manager:** reported the consortium's fall from over 34% to ~6%, and "We have also aligned executive compensation with the median of our global sector peer group" (AGM 2024). Remuneration passed with ~11% dissent (AR 2024).

### Anna Manz — CFO Nov 2020 → Feb 2024
The most candid voice in the corpus.
- "Much of what we've achieved over the last three years has been about fixing the basics and running an undermanaged business better." (2023-11-16)
- "historically investment has not always kept pace with technology change." (AR 2022)
- "every time I say that NTI will probably decline in the subsequent quarter, I've been wrong so far" (2022-10-21)
- "recoveries are a bit of a red herring" (2021-07-02)

Manz left for Nestlé after the CMD the CFO had shaped.

### Michel-Alain Proch ("MAP") — CFO from Feb 2024
- **Agenda:** operating leverage, insourcing ~2,000 contractors, a fortnightly Group Investment Committee, zero-based budgeting.
- "Now it's time to execute." (2024-04-25)
- "It's not rocket science. It requires discipline and attention." (2024-08-01)
- "this is something I have delivered before and am confident we can deliver at LSEG." (AR 2024)
- **On AI:** "we are not in the business of buying chips for billions of dollars." (2025-11-10) and "MCP, for sure, will be monetized." (2026-07-30)
- **Changed the KPIs:** demoted ASV ("over time it will become less and less relevant as we move towards more consumption-based pricing", 2024-04-25), then retired it.

### The Data & Analytics heads (2021–2026)
- **David Craig** (Refinitiv CEO; left 2021). Before the close Craig had set out the data thesis: "Our aim is to capture the opportunity of data which we believe is driving unprecedented change in the global financial community." (2019-08-01)
- **Andrea Remyn Stone** (ex-Lehman, McGraw-Hill, S&P, Bloomberg, Dealogic; Jul 2021 – Jun 2022). Built the Workspace counter-positioning: "Workspace is not a closed box, one size fits all terminal."
- **Satvinder Singh** (ex-Mastercard; 2023–24). Positioned D&A on a co-innovation story with Microsoft. "Nobody does everything we do." and "while the opportunity is there, it will not last forever." (2023-11-17)
- **Ron Lefferts** (ex-sales head) **+ Gianluca Biagini** (ex-S&P Global Market Intelligence; co-founder of Bloomberg Data Solutions per AR 2025). "Our focus is on delivering trusted, multi-asset class content and workflow solutions that function as strategic infrastructure." (Lefferts, 2025-12-16). "Our ambition is simple - to be the leading provider of trusted data and actionable insights for customers." (Biagini, 2025-11-10)

### Daniel Maguire — Post Trade, then Head of Markets
The utility thinker.
- "nobody wants to buy the world’s cheapest parachute." (2024-10-01)
- "the uncleared market is, to a large extent, white space." (2024-10-01)
- Architect of the 2025 PTS deal ([[lseg-post-trade-clearing]]).
- Isabelle Girolami (CEO LCH Ltd, later replaced by Susi de Verdelon): "Our EBITDA margin is around 50% and we think it's pretty healthy." (2024-10-01)

### Julia Hoggett — CEO, London Stock Exchange plc (ex-FCA)
Policy advocate for UK listings and tokenisation.
- "Strengthening our financial ecosystem and thereby improving the competitiveness of the UK capital markets is a continuous task." (2022-07-19)
- "Climate change is the most urgent challenge of our lifetimes." (2021-11-05)

### Fiona Bassett — CEO FTSE Russell (ex-DWS)
- "If Bloomberg is a walled garden, we aspire to be the open meadow." (2024-12-10)
- "FTSE Russell's country classification framework is grounded in the principle of accessibility to global financial markets." (2025-10-07)

### Irfan Hussain (CIO) and Emily Prince (Head of AI)
- Hussain: "We are a fintech firm, and you cannot build amazing products without the best engineers." and "We don't think of ourselves in the model game." (2025-11-10)
- Prince runs the MCP/AI distribution: "LSEG does not run the customer's model inference or control token usage." (2026-07-17)

### Others with a distinct voice
- **Murray Roos** (Capital Markets): "Our ambition is to be the first global exchange group that is genuinely indifferent as to whether a company is public or private" (2022-03-16)
- **Dean Berry** (Trading & Banking) on the Refinitiv legacy, and **Neill Penney** (FX) on underinvestment: "punching below its weight due to old and underperforming technology" (Matching, 2021-10-01).
- **David Wilson** (Risk Intelligence): "So yes, we're the oldest, so we'll get the legacy tag." (2025-06-17) and "this is data that learns." (2026-09-28)
- **Peregrine Riviere** (IR) sets the call rules: one question plus one follow-up. On the Post Trade webinar: "This is very much a teach-in rather than a mini Capital Market Day." (2024-10-01)
- **Board-level outsiders.** Scott Guthrie (Microsoft): "The combination of LSEG’s data platform and Microsoft’s AI capabilities will unlock tremendous new potential for our customers." (AR 2023). Matt Kerner (Microsoft, guest): "This partnership is a game changer for us." (2025-11-10)

## 4. Communication style

- **Schwimmer** repeats the same nouns (open, partner, breadth, trusted) across seven years of releases. On calls Schwimmer rebuts directly and rarely gives numbers ahead of the CFO. "We're not going to give anything more explicit" is a common close.
- **Manz** was the explainer, good at decomposing ASV and NTI. **Proch** is the programme manager, with targets, a "cockpit", and the margin bridge.
- **The IR craft:** strict one-question rules, teach-in webinars for each division (Post Trade 2024, FTSE Russell 2024, Risk Intelligence 2025), and the Innovation Forum (2025) used to answer a narrative attack.

## 5. Leadership lenses

- **Succession and outside hires** ([[canon-grove-high-output-management]], [[canon-larson-elegant-puzzle]]): five D&A leaders in five years is a structural choice about the hardest division, not bad luck. Each hire came with a mandate from a different rival.
- **Narrative and persuasion** ([[canon-duarte-resonate]], [[canon-minto-pyramid-principle]]): the Innovation Forum is a textbook *what is vs what could be* construction.
- **Candor** ([[canon-scott-radical-candor]]): Manz's "fixing the basics" and the 2021 investor-day admissions are rare executive candour. Compare Tradeweb's guarded voice ([[tradeweb-people]]).
- **Power** ([[canon-pfeffer-power]]): a 37% seller with board seats, then Microsoft's board seat. Who has influence shifted as the register changed.
