# Reference · Entrepreneurship — the Operator's Playbook

> Practitioner-sourced synthesis (Lenny's Podcast, YC, Hormozi, Reddit founder threads, investor talks).
> The *operational* layer beneath the canon's conceptual books — maps to **Course 10 (Entrepreneurship &
> Venture)** and the **Product Thinking & Innovation** cluster ([[canon-ries-lean-startup]],
> [[canon-thiel-zero-to-one]], [[canon-cagan-inspired]]). Banked 2026-06-06.

## 1 · Getting to product-market fit
- **"Interesting" is a polite no.** Only "wow" + behavior counts — *"I'd sign up today,"* *"can we meet
  next week?"* Reinhardt (Segment): 4 of 5 "that's great, makes sense" = doesn't care.
- **Willingness-to-pay = actually charge**, raising price until they wince but still pay (Ramanujam's
  fair→expensive→prohibitive ladder; "expensive" is the real number). Reinhardt asked $120K, closed $18K.
- **Best customer is already failing to build the fix themselves** — has budget, is shopping vendors.
  Ironclad's break: one-line cold email *"Are you a CLM?"* to someone evaluating 10–12 tools.
- **Sell before you build.** Lattice sold its first 5–10 on Figma mockups; Vanta manually filled a
  spreadsheet pretending to be the AI. *Extreme satisfaction first, efficiency later* (Cagan-style discovery).
- **Pick the wedge where customers hack your product**, not where your vision points. Segment found its
  real product when 5 customers independently described the same unprompted workflow.
- **Quant bar:** Sean Ellis test — **40% "very disappointed"** if it vanished. Track cohort renewal + NRR,
  not signups. *"$1M ARR a month after launch is ZERO proof of PMF."*
- **When stuck, 200% pivot, not 10%** — change Persona / Problem / Promise / Product. Plaid kept its code,
  flipped persona consumers→fintech devs. *(See [[canon-ries-lean-startup]] pivot-or-persevere.)*

## 2 · Founding team & early hires
- **The killer is relationship, not skill.** YC: never hears "my cofounder lacks skills"; always "my
  cofounder is a nightmare." Pick someone you've fought with and like over a perfect-skill stranger.
- **Avoid 50/50 deadlock** — near-equal but give the CEO one extra share as a written tiebreaker.
- **Always vest:** 4 years, 1-year cliff. Else a cofounder who quits month 8 keeps a frozen cap-table slice.
- **Too little conflict is the silent killer.** Garry Tan resigned from Posterous (10× growth) at resting
  pulse 120 because he and his cofounder stopped talking. Tell: you don't hang out outside work.
- **"Fighting about everything" = undefined roles.** Heroku gave every task *one owner whose ass was on the
  line*, then disagree-and-commit. *(Echoes [[canon-bezos-shareholder-letters]] / [[canon-alchian-demsetz-firm]].)*
- **Equity only for money, skills, or time you genuinely lack.** Hormozi (9 failed partnerships): run a
  prospective partner through lawyers/contract first as a stress test.

## 3 · Unit economics before scale
- **LTV uses lifetime gross profit, not revenue.** Hormozi: a $2,000/mo client netted $0 after ad + mgmt.
- **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1** (near-universal). Below = costs too high or churn too fast.
- **CAC payback < 12 mo (ideally 6–9).** Even 3:1 bankrupts you if payback exceeds runway.
- **Formulas:** LTV = (ARPU × gross margin) ÷ churn; payback = CAC ÷ (ARPU × gross margin). Target **65%+
  gross margin (80%+ SaaS).** *(Ties to `learn/unit-04` health + [[canon-klarman-margin-of-safety]].)*
- **Watch contribution margin.** Uber was **−$0.22/ride** after driver pay/incentives — "make it up on
  volume" is the trap.
- **Price is the highest-leverage lever:** a ~10% price bump barely dents conversion, falls straight to
  profit (one case: profit/customer $40→$64, +63%). *(See [[canon-shapiro-varian-information-rules]].)*

## 4 · Distribution & go-to-market
- **First 100 come from personal network (51%) + 1:1 outreach (50%).** SEO/content (~9%), Product Hunt (~4%)
  rarely produce *early* traction. **YC:** "startups don't take off by themselves — manually recruit."
- **Match channel to user.** Cold email to tradespeople ≈ 4% reply; texts/Nextdoor/local FB/BNI work.
  Creator/affiliate endorsements convert ~10× cold for low-trust audiences.
- **First 20–30 sales calls = research, not pitches** — find the one-sentence trigger that makes prospects
  "light up." One founder: 0→sale-in-11-days after recutting ICP to "solo founders who just hired their
  first salesperson."
- **Frustrated users are highest-converting** — reply personally, fix the bug same-day.
- **Data, not pitches, earns attention** — *"only 7 of 2,500 agency sites scored above 90"* outperformed
  daily promo. SEO compounds only if you write to the panic-moment query.

## 5 · When/whether to raise (or not)
- **VC needs 100–1,000×.** A profitable $5M/yr business is a *failure* by their math — *"don't put jet fuel
  in a Toyota Camry."* *(See [[canon-bezos-shareholder-letters]] capital allocation.)*
- **Dilution can invert outcomes:** two bootstrappers split a $50M exit → $25M each; after YC+seed+A+B, a
  $200M exit pays each only ~$20M.
- **"Google rule":** only raise when you literally can't exist otherwise. Raising sells freedom.
- **Below ~50% ownership (≈Series B) the board can set your salary and fire you** (Kalanick is structural,
  not a freak event). *(See [[canon-jensen-meckling-agency-theory]] control.)*
- **Deadliest failure = "between-rounds death"** — pre-seed must fund you to a fundable milestone + buffer.
- **VC incentive is misaligned** — they want you to "light capital on fire"; ~2 of 20 bets carry the fund.
- **Counterpoint:** only ~6% of Fortune 500 were VC-funded; non-dilutive capital is an underused middle path.

## 6 · Operational scaling pain
- **"If you build it, they will come" is the #1 scaling killer** — you're in the sales business first.
- **Overbuilding before PMF > underbuilding.** Scope creep disguised as iteration (widget → analytics
  platform in 5 weeks, never nailing one job) silently kills. *(Anti-pattern to [[canon-cagan-inspired]].)*
- Self-inflicted wounds: limiting hiring to your own country; pitching investors before traction.

## 7 · Moat & defensibility
- **Track the moat's *direction*, not its size today.** Only widening gross margin / pricing power / cohort
  engagement is durable. *"If you can spot it easily, it probably doesn't exist."* *(See [[canon-helmer-7-powers]].)*
- **Ultimate moat: deliberately crash your own gross margin** so no competitor can profitably enter
  (Amazon Basics, Jio). **Virality and capital are NOT moats** (Clubhouse had both, ~zero switching costs).
- **Build the demand side; supply gets sucked in.** Canva (high standalone utility) → $6B while Shutterstock
  (utility tied to supply) → ~$1.7B. *(See [[canon-parker-platform-revolution]], [[canon-hagiu-wright-multi-sided-platforms]].)*
- **Underrated buildable moats:** customer-controlled switching costs; concierge service (Zappos).

## 8 · The other seat — how VCs think (the investor-side mirror)
Everything above is the **founder's** seat. The **allocator's** seat is in **[[harlem-capital-vc-insights]]**
(Henri Pierre-Jacques / Brandon Bryant, Harlem Capital — 17 videos). The pairings that matter most:
- **"Is it venture?"** — VCs reject great cash-flow businesses as "not venture" (need $100M–$1B+ via
  IPO/acquisition + 50%+ growth). A "no" is often a *classification*, not a judgment. *(↔ §5.)*
- **Fund size dictates the check** — sub-$50M funds run 5–8% ownership; $100M+ funds *must* own 10–20%, so a
  small check from a big fund breaks *their* math. Match your raise to their portfolio construction. *(↔ §5.)*
- **LPs buy process over a lucky home run; carry is $0 if the fund misses full-carry mode** (European
  waterfall: LPs whole first → 8% hurdle → catch-up → 80/20). Markups are leading indicators that can
  evaporate 70%. This is *why* VCs push for 100–1,000× and "light capital on fire." *(↔ §5.)*
- **Brand/trust is sourcing** — VCs "track founders for years before they have a company"; be the first
  call. "Document, don't create." *(↔ "distribution is the new moat" below.)*

## Two cross-cutting patterns
- **Distribution is the new moat** — AI makes the product easy; getting attention is the defensible part.
- **Sell the outcome, not the mechanism** — *"post a job photo, Google finds your next customer"* beat
  *"feed-first website builder with auto-indexing."* Same product, opposite conversion.
  *(See [[canon-sinek-start-with-why]], [[canon-duarte-resonate]].)*
