## G. Themes and surprises

- **Private markets is the strategic through-line of 2025–26.** Sequence: Daphne investment (Feb 2025) → Hamilton Lane indices (Oct 2025) → MAC3 private-fund models (Apr 2026) → Canoe integration (Apr 2026) → DLEN direct-lending data (Apr 2026) → Canoe acquisition (Jul/Oct 2026). The integration-then-acquire pattern (partner in April, buy in July) is a template. Bloomberg's own count: 3M private companies, 50,000 private funds, 16,000 direct loans.
- **AI messaging shifts from "summaries" to "data readiness" and agents.** 2024: earnings-call summaries; 2025: news summaries, Document Insights, RAG-safety papers; 2026: ASKB ("agentic AI conversational interface", "coordinated AI agents"), Enterprise MCP, "Skills". Bloomberg argues its edge is metadata/entitlements, not models — BloombergGPT is never mentioned.
- **Bloomberg is helping write the agent protocol.** Engineers are "primary authors" of MCP enterprise-governance proposals; Bloomberg "started and leads" the MCP Financial Services Interest Group and sits on the Agentic AI Foundation board (2026-09-29). Earlier: Envoy AI Gateway with Tetrate under CNCF (2025-02-25). Open-standard play parallels FIGI.
- **The venue strategy is "protocol by protocol, market by market".** MOC (EGB → JGB), list/BWIC (ABS), EFP (Australia), spread-to-benchmark PT, FX options RFQ API, IGB via NDS-OM, Swap Connect, RMB repo. Many are framed as "first" trades with a named dealer/client. Electronic Markets claims 175 markets / 9,000 client firms.
- **Index business is pivoting from yardstick to product factory**: custom/QIS indices for ETFs (Amplify TLTP, F/m RBIL, Fideuram DXDC), FIAs (SILAC BVERSA10, $30bn AUM tracking in annuity/life), crypto blends, and the appointment of a QIS veteran as BISL CEO. Equity index mandate wins (Triodos, Canaccord, Metrobank, Touchstone) are framed as cross-sell from fixed income.
- **Tokenization stance is conservative**: license data onto chains (Kaiko/Canton), dashboards (Allium), regulatory classification (Basel crypto). No tokenized-asset trading, no stablecoin issuance, no deposit tokens. Bitcoin pricing on Terminal "since 2014".
- **Asia/China is heavily over-represented** (~15 items: HK Family Office Nexus, GBA talent, Bond Connect, Swap Connect, Guotai Haitong, Maxwealth QDII, Metrobank, MSB Vietnam, MUFG, India, Australia). Tone is closely aligned with HK/PRC policy goals.
- **Surprise — boilerplate stats contradict each other** (dealers 1,500 vs 800; PORT Enterprise client counts; AIM/PORT shared clients 200/300/200; B500 market cap "$58.19 trillion" repeated unchanged from Aug 2025 to Mar 2026). Don't cite these numbers without checking date.
- **Surprise — the corporate "About Bloomberg" splinters in 2026**: Enterprise Data and trading-analytics releases drop "transparency, efficiency, and fairness" and "news", suggesting business-line-specific positioning (data vendor, trading tech) rather than one media-and-data identity. BI releases still use a pre-2020s variant.
- **Surprise — Bloomberg's AI research undercuts generic AI hype**: its own paper finds RAG makes LLMs less safe (2025-04-28), and LQA is framed as built "At a time when skepticism around AI … was widespread" (2025-09-16). Responsible-AI framing is a sales tool for regulated clients (Vault "transparent and well documented").
