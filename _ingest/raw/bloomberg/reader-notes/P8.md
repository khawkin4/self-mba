## G. Themes and surprises

- Surprise: this partition contains almost no first-person statements from Bloomberg executives; the "people" are product managers. The strategic voice comes from Foster/Phillips (private markets), Sharaby/Atha/Gendron (indices), Krein/Kazi (execution/pricing).
- Vertical integration is the story of https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/trading/how-tradable-products-and-modern-market-infrastructure-are-reshaping-credit-markets/: IBVAL AI reference price -> Tradable Trackers -> PTBB -> credit index futures (CME/Eurex) executed on EMSX/RFQe, all benchmarked to Bloomberg indices. Pricing, index IP, execution and exchange-licensing in one loop is an unusual market-structure position vs Tradeweb/MarketAxess.
- IBVAL is "now the default pricing source on the Bloomberg Terminal" (2026-02-18) - a shift from end-of-day BVAL to intraday AI model pricing; refresh 15 seconds (corporates) / 1 second (govvies); not stated when the switch occurred.
- Candour about the Agg: Bloomberg's own research says most active-fund "alpha" is credit beta and many are misbenchmarked to the Agg (https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/financial-services/behind-the-benchmark-dissecting-active-bond-fund-performance-2/), then launches TOTALFI a week later (https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/trading/bloomberg-indices-launches-bloomberg-us-total-fixed-income-market-index-to-capture-the-full-investable-us-fixed-income-universe/). Inference: product is a response to benchmark mismatch.
- Gold weight: BCOM gold observed weight peaked at 20.48% vs 15% cap (target weights are reset annually in January), showing the methodology's slow reconstitution is by design (https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/markets/cocoa-returns-to-bcom-for-2026/).
- Contradiction/unclear numbers: ETF market is "$15 trillion" (Nov 2025, https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/markets/how-is-thematic-investing-reshaping-equity-markets/) vs "almost $19 trillion" (Feb 2026, https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/trading/how-tradable-products-and-modern-market-infrastructure-are-reshaping-credit-markets/) - different dates/definitions; private credit "~$2tn" (https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/markets/bdc-issued-debt-a-public-window-into-private-credit-issuers/) vs "$1.7 trillion" direct lending (https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/financial-services/a-changing-landscape-for-private-credit/) vs "$41 trillion addressable" (https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/markets/41-trillion-credit-market-how-is-private-credit-reshaping-the-landscape/); BCOM gold weight 14.29% start-2025 vs 14.90% target 2026.
