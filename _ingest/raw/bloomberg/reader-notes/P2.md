## G. Themes and surprises

- **The credit/rates execution build-out is quiet but real.** Bridge (all-to-all, 2022), RBLD automation (rates doubled), the Broadway acquisition (low-latency rates market making, closed Oct 2023), and IBVAL Front Office (15-second ML pricing) together form a complete electronification stack for the sell side and buy side. There is no FIT/BSEF/BMTF venue news at all. Bloomberg describes its execution push as technology and workflow, not as venues.
- **Index + pricing convergence.** Tradable Trackers (2022) and IBVAL FO (2023) explicitly link BISL indices and BVAL pricing into derivatives and portfolio-trading products. That is the same playbook as the ICE/MarketAxess/Tradeweb data businesses.
- **Consolidated tape arc is complete in-partition.** Explore (Jun 2022) → sign (May 2023) → abandon (Dec 2023). The abandonment cites "product definitions" and "structural complexities". Also notable: the final statement adds the UK (never mentioned in the 2022/May 2023 texts) and is hosted by Tradeweb.
- **ESG saturation.** About 30 of 95 items are ESG/climate products or surveys. Bloomberg markets itself as the regulatory-data utility for SFDR, EU Taxonomy, PAB and CSRD. Coverage rises steeply: carbon data 50,000 cos (Jun 2022) → 100,000 (Oct 2022) → 130,000 (Dec 2023); company-reported ESG 13,000 → 15,000 → 15,500. The late-2023 BI survey admits ESG "has come under pressure".
- **BloombergGPT came as a paper, not a product.** In 2023 the AI story is research credibility plus a data-moat argument, followed by BI's "$1.3 trillion" GenAI market sizing (2023-06-01). No GenAI Terminal feature launches in this partition.
- **Boilerplate pivot (mid-2022).** The About Bloomberg text drops the news-leader / core-of-the-Terminal language for "transparency, efficiency, and fairness to markets" and "the global financial ecosystem". The identity moves from information vendor to market-infrastructure steward. Old text lingers into 2023.
- **Growth geography = Asia.** India (RBL, HDFC, WhiteOak, ABSLAMC/GIFT City, Apex, NJ AM), Japan (Mizuho, All Nippon, SuMi Trust, FFG), China/HK (Swap Connect, BSE, ZA Bank, GBA), plus a central-bank venue in Azerbaijan. Enterprise sales outside the US/EU are a large share of client releases.
- **Number drift / internal inconsistencies:**
  - Data License fields "more than 15 thousand fields" (2023-04-11) vs "40,000 data fields" (2023-06-28).
  - AIM AUM "$17 trillion" (Dec 2022–Feb 2023) → "$22 trillion" (Aug–Nov 2023), with the same "nearly 15,000 professionals at over 900 client firms".
  - BVAL coverage 2.5m (Jul 2022) → 2.7m (Jul 2023) → 2.8m (Oct 2023).

  - IBVAL is "BVAL’s intraday pricing product" (2022), then "IBVAL Front Office is a new offering, distinct from but complementary to" BVAL (2023), and also the Tradable Trackers engine.
