# Reader notes, P1: Bloomberg press releases 2019–2021 (101 items)

Every item was read in full. What this partition covers: the "Bloomberg Professional Services" and press-announcement newsroom from Jan 2019 to Dec 2021. Most items are product, index or venue releases. The rest are sustainability/CFLI pieces, DEI and philanthropy releases, and Bloomberg Intelligence (BI) survey releases. There is no AI-model news (BloombergGPT came later), no consolidated-tape news, and no tokenization beyond crypto FIGIs.

## G. Themes and surprises

- **China is the single largest commercial storyline** (~15 items). Bloomberg holds three roles at once: index provider deciding inclusion (Agg), trading gateway (Bond Connect, CIBM Direct, Southbound via TOMS) and pricing source (BVAL on CFETS). The China voices are uniformly upbeat. The only caution comes from ICMA and the HKMA.
- **Rates-transition infrastructure is the second spine**: €STR → SOFR compression → BSBY → ISDA fallback calculations → surveys. Bloomberg is at once venue (BSEF/BMTF), benchmark administrator (BISL/BSBY) and fallback calculator. That is unusual vertical integration in benchmark plumbing.
- **The index business is being built into a multi-asset challenger**: rebrand of BRAIS (2016) completed 2021. Equity added 2019, EM/thematic 2021. BCOM "acquisition" 2020. Multi-asset 2020. "crypto" named as an index asset class (2021-08-24 https://www.bloomberg.com/company/press/bloomberg-completes-fixed-income-indices-rebrand/). BISL's regulated status (2019) is the base.
- **ESG overshadows AI**: roughly 25 items touch ESG and sustainability. "AI" appears only in passing (a BWBN report and an Israeli bank's own AI). BQuant Enterprise (2021) and the alt-data push (EAP, Second Measure) are the forerunners of the later AI story.
- **Surprise: Bloomberg rates ESG and resells its rivals' ratings at the same time** (own scores Aug 2020, MSCI Oct 2020, Sustainalytics Oct 2021). This is a platform-not-product posture.
- **Surprise: the FIGI team's openness rhetoric** ("free of charge with no material impediments on use"; "without performance and cost restraints") is an unstated challenge to the CUSIP/ISIN licensing model. Crypto FIGIs via Kaiko (Oct 2021) are the only digital-asset item.
- **Internal contradictions and stat drift**:
 - Enterprise Access Point is called "first launched in 2018" in the same release (2020-09-16 https://www.bloomberg.com/company/press/bloomberg-expands-alternative-data-offering/) that says alternative-data vendors tripled "since the product’s introduction in February 2019".
