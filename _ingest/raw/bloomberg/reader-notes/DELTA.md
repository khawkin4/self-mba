## 3. Findings that change or contradict existing conclusions

(a) "Bloomberg never names Tradeweb, MarketAxess or ICE": HOLDS for Tradeweb and MarketAxess and LSEG/Refinitiv (zero mentions in 104 items). QUALIFIED for ICE: the Oct 2026 Treasury-clearing piece names "ICE Clear Credit" (and CME Securities Clearing and FICC) as registered clearing agencies, a neutral factual mention of a clearing house, not a trading-venue competitor. Also named: CME Group (EBS, BFIX partner/data source), S&P Global (as a source of insurance premiums forecast in BI note; S&P 500 ESG Index in Tesla note; as rating agency in a UOB release), Nasdaq/SGX (APAC outlook), Jane Street, CFETS, FICC. So: Bloomberg names market infrastructure when it is a partner, data input or regulatory fact; it still does not name venue rivals.

(b) "Regulatory briefs never mention the abandoned 2023 consolidated-tape JV": HOLDS. Zero uses of "consolidated tape" or even "tape" in any item, including the Aug 2024 Trading and Markets regulatory brief, the Nov 2025 and Jun 2025 risk briefs, and the APAC 2026 outlook. Absence is notable because the Oct 2026 clearing article and Aug 2024 brief do cover US/UK/EU market-structure rules (UK listing rules, ESMA CSDR, SEC/CFTC rules) without the tape topic.

(c) "Tokenization strategy = license data onto chains, not run venues": SUPPORTED, strengthened. The 30 Sept 2026 stablecoin release says Bloomberg is "providing the data, analytics and connectivity institutions need as tokenized markets become integrated with traditional finance"; it cites a "recent collaboration with Kaiko to make Bloomberg Data License content available on the Canton Network" and Allium onchain data into the Terminal (RWAS <GO>). Crypto stance in indices (Bitcoin/Gold blends) is also an index-licensing play. Nuance: Bloomberg does run real venues, but only in traditional assets and often as a gateway (Swap Connect/CFETS, SEF, RFQe, FXGO, BSKT, a central-bank Auction System). Tokenization in the regulatory briefs (BNM discussion paper, HK tokenized bonds) is tracked as regulation only.

(d) "About Bloomberg boilerplate shifted mid-2022 to transparency, efficiency, and fairness": CONFIRMED and narrowed. In this partition the old text ("Bloomberg, the global business and financial information and news leader, gives influential decision makers a critical edge by connecting them to a dynamic network of information, people and ideas.") appears from 2019-03-27 (and a "Bloomberg LP, the global ..." form on 2019-12-09) through 2022-01-20. The new text ("Bloomberg is a global leader in business and financial information, delivering trusted data, news, and insights that bring transparency, efficiency, and fairness to markets. The company helps connect influential communities across the global financial ecosystem ...") first appears 2022-07-19 and runs to 2026-09-30. The switch therefore happened between 20 Jan 2022 and 19 Jul 2022. Items in the gap (9 and 18 Mar 2022) carry only the BISL index boilerplate. Minor variants: Oxford commas dropped in the Sept 2026 stablecoin release; Terminal boilerplate "has revolutionized" (Jun 2025) vs "has transformed" (Sept 2026); AI boilerplate "Since 2009, Bloomberg has been building and using artificial intelligence" gains "agentic AI architectures" by Sept 2026.

Other tension: Kleinbauer's title is given three ways in one 2026 article and differs from 2023, a sign of role renaming. Bloomberg describes itself in the Treasury-clearing piece as "well positioned" while also stating much done-away infrastructure "is still being developed" (ADMITTED gap).
