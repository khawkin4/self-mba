Supply Chain Analysis
SPLC <GO> is another widely used fundamental Bloomberg function that show a company’s
Suppliers and Customers. The Revenue and Cost feature under each supplier represents the
Revenue and Cost incurred from the listed stock. The Revenue and Cost incurred under each
customer implies the Revenue and Cost that the listed company (UA) receives from that
customer. This is one of if not the best functions within Bloomberg to view what other
companies that a company utilizes and their exposure to each one.




*Click on any company other than the main one viewed will easily change to
that company’s supply chain.
