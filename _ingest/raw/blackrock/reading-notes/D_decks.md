## 1. Record list

- **2023-06-14 | BlackRock 2023 Investor Day presentation**: 138-slide deck built around the "Platform as a Service" pitch. Covers the 5% organic base fee growth ambition, Aladdin's 2022 tech revenue ($1.4B), the iShares 2030 targets (ETF industry $25T; fixed income ETFs $2.5T), doubling private markets revenue in 5 years, and a $100B+ transition platform.
- **2024-01-12 | BlackRock to Acquire GIP – investor presentation**: $3B cash plus ~12M shares (nominal ~$12.55B) for Global Infrastructure Partners. Creates the #2 private infrastructure manager ($150B+). The founders lead the combined platform and Bayo Ogunlesi joins the Board.
- **2024-07-01 (deck dated "June 30, 2024") | BlackRock to Acquire Preqin – investor presentation**: £2.55B (~$3.2B) cash for Preqin. Pitched as the "first end-to-end provider in the private markets" (investments, technology and data), with Preqin integrated into Aladdin/eFront, benchmarks and indices, and AI.
- **2024-12-03 | BlackRock to Acquire HPS – investor presentation**: $12B, paid 100% in exchangeable "BLK SubCo units", for HPS Investment Partners. Creates a top-five private credit manager (~$220B). HPS founders join the Global Executive Committee.
- **2025-06-12 | BlackRock 2025 Investor Day presentation**: "2030 Ambition". Aims to double operating income and market cap to $15B and $280B. Targets: ≥5% organic base fee growth, ≥45% margin, $400B private markets fundraising 2025–2030, and 30%+ of revenue from private markets plus technology. Also covers digital assets/IBIT, Aladdin plus Preqin, and the HPS/GIP integration.

## 2. Key events (dated)

1. **2021 Investor Day (referenced in the 2023 deck)**: set a "$100B+ 3–year gross fundraise target from ’21–’23" in private markets. By the 2023 deck, $82B had been raised and the slide says it was "on track" (*2023 Investor Day*).
2. **2019 (referenced)**: eFront acquired "to bring private markets into Aladdin". By 2023, 40% of new Aladdin clients used eFront (*2023 Investor Day*). The 2025 deck reports eFront ACV at 2x since acquisition.
3. **2023-06-14**: Investor Day. Sets a **5% organic base fee growth target for 2023–2027**, with industry growth projected at 3% and BlackRock's share of industry base fees rising from 2.8% to 3.1%. Also targets **doubling private markets revenue in 5 years**, an Aladdin "Low to mid–teen long–term growth target", and iShares targets for 2030 (*2023 Investor Day*).
4. **2Q/3Q 2023 (referenced)**: minority investment in Avaloq and acquisition of Kreos Capital, both pending at the time (*2023 Investor Day*, slide 129).
5. **2024-01-12**: GIP deal announced. Expected close 3Q 2024 (*GIP deck*). The 2025 deck shows it closed: "6.9 million shares issued on October 1, 2024 as part of the GIP acquisition close".
6. **2024-01-11 (referenced)**: IBIT spot bitcoin ETP launch window. The 2025 deck's trading-share data runs "from January 11, 2024" (*2025 Investor Day*).
7. **May 2024 (referenced)**: SpiderRock acquisition closed. It was integrated into a "BLK for Options" suite (*2025 Investor Day*).
8. **2024-06-30/07-01**: Preqin deal announced (*Preqin deck*). It closed **March 3, 2025**, per "Since Preqin close on March 3rd" (*2025 Investor Day*).
9. **2024-12-03**: HPS deal announced, with expected close "mid-2025" (*HPS deck*). It was still "Pending close" at the June 2025 Investor Day, yet HPS CEO Scott Kapnick presented there (*2025 Investor Day*).
10. **By June 2025**: **GIP V** closed above its $25B target, the "largest-ever client capital raise in an infrastructure fund". BlackRock also announced the **AI Infrastructure Partnership (AIP)** with hyperscalers and energy partners, reached an "In-principle agreement alongside partners MSC and TiL" on ports, and returned $9B to LPs since the GIP close (*2025 Investor Day*).
11. **2025-03 to 05**: **BUIDL**, the tokenized liquidity fund built with Securitize, reached $2B (as of 4/9/2025). Circle USDC reserves managed by BlackRock: $50B+. An iShares European Bitcoin ETP launched in Q1 2025 (*2025 Investor Day*).
12. **2025-06-12**: Investor Day sets the **2030 aspirations**: revenue $35B+ (ex-beta), operating income ~2x to $15B, market cap 2x to $280B, private markets plus technology at 30%+ of revenue, and **$400B cumulative private markets gross fundraising from 2025 to 2030**. The ≥5% organic base fee growth and ≥45% operating margin targets are restated as through-the-cycle targets (*2025 Investor Day*).
13. **2025 (referenced)**: JioBlackRock (India) joint venture with Jio Financial Services. Also a "$5B anchor investment in BRIM" (BlackRock Riyadh Investment Management) and a "$30B capital target" for "Partnering on global AI investing with the UAE" (*2025 Investor Day*).

## 6. Tailwinds / headwinds / challenges

**Tailwinds**
- **Manager consolidation:** "Clients are consolidating to trusted providers, with room to grow" (2023). In 2025, "Top 5 asset managers have 25% AUM market share" and BLK's share rose from 7% to 8% (2018 to 2023). ADMITTED (company claim).
- **ETF growth:**
  - 2023 forecast: industry $25T by 2030.
  - 2025 forecast: $27T by 2030 at a 15% CAGR. ETFs are only 6% of global public markets, 10% of equity and 2% of fixed income.
  - Fixed income ETFs "hit $2.5T in 2024" industry-wide but "remain just 2% of total bond market".
  - ADMITTED.
- **Private markets and private credit:**
  - Private credit AUM forecast at "$4.5T+" by 2030E. "Institutions are underallocated to private debt targets by ~200bps (5.7% vs. target 7.8%)". Retail private debt is $116B and up 30%+ since 2022.
  - Private markets data TAM is forecast at $18B by 2030, growing 12% a year (Preqin deck).
  - Infrastructure funding need: $75T (2022–2040, GIP deck). The 2025 deck says $94T of needs by 2040 and a $15T gap.
  - Data centers need ~$1.5T+ by 2030.
  - ADMITTED.
- **Bank retrenchment:** "Less support from banks as capital requirements continue to rise"; "Basel III endgame" (HPS deck). ADMITTED.
- **Higher-rate regime:** helps fixed income and active management. Rieder (2023 and 2025) cites dispersion; in 2025, "Dispersion today is over 145% higher compared to 20181". ADMITTED.
- **Crypto ETPs:** IBIT has $48B in net inflows, held ~70% of spot BTC ETP trading in 2025, and 75% of direct IBIT buyers were first-time iShares users. ADMITTED.

**Headwinds / admissions**
- **2022 beta shock:**
  - 2023 deck: "Differentiated organic growth and margin over the long–term, with near–term results impacted by historic market headwinds".
  - 2022 revenue fell 8% to $17.9B; margin fell 400bps to 42.8%; AUM fell 14% to $8.6T; markets/FX knocked off ($1,722B) in 2022.
  - The 2025 deck calls 41.7% the "Market cycle low in 2023".
  - ADMITTED.
- **Missed private markets fundraising target:** the 2021 target of $100B+ for '21–'23 stood at $82B raised through 1Q23, labeled "on track". INFERRED: likely short of the target.
- **Fee-rate pressure:** "Fee rate is an output of client preferences and markets" (2025); slide 9 says new builds are "Driving positive leverage to BlackRock fee rate". Core equity ETFs earn 8 bps and cash 2–3 bps. INFERRED: the mix is diluting fees, which explains why BlackRock is pushing private markets and technology to 30%+ of revenue.
- **Shrinking active category:** Systematic is "Aiming to grow share in a large, shrinking category" (2025, Savi). ADMITTED.
- **Fixed income:** "We’ve consistently outpaced industry flows in a flat to negative fixed income market environment" (2025). ADMITTED.
- **Deal dilution and leverage:**
  - Preqin is "modestly dilutive to as-adjusted earnings and operating margin in 2025", with Debt/EBITDA rising to ~1.4x and up to $3B of incremental debt.
  - GIP: $3B of cash "funded by incremental debt".
  - HPS: units at closing are "~6% of outstanding shares".
  - ADMITTED.
- **Cost synergies minimal at GIP:** "Not a primary rationale". ADMITTED.
- **Conflicts:** HPS deck says "balance sheet-lite scale avoids conflict with key client base". Preqin deck promises "safeguarding GP/LP data through information barriers". ADMITTED as risks being managed. INFERRED: owning Preqin while being a large GP creates a neutrality and data-conflict risk.
- **Insurance regulation:** "Evolving landscape with growing regulatory constraints" (2025 Aladdin insurance case). ADMITTED.
- **Risk-factor additions:** the 2024 deal decks add "(12) the failure to effectively manage the development and use of AI" and "increasing focus from stakeholders regarding ESG matters". The 2025 deck adds "global trade tensions, tariffs". ADMITTED.

## 8. Market-structure links

- **BlackRock as a client of dealers and venues (execution economics):**
  - 2023: "25% Savings in fixed income execution costs vs. market average". The methodology: FI benchmark = "previous day’s closing price"; "Market Half Spread … based on a consensus opinion of BlackRock traders as well as a set of over 10 broker dealers".
  - Rieder 2023: "6600 fixed income trades each day", "8400+ primary market sourcing opportunities annually", "$87B of capital invested in primary issuance", "2x Average fill rates pro–rata allocations" (IG credit new issues).
- **Internalization (the key 2025 signal for Tradeweb):**
  - Goldstein 2025: "25% of equity trades offset in 2024 for clients" and "~$600 million in transaction costs saved for clients in 2024 via internalization and new scaled fixed income capability".
  - Savi: "Decrease reliance on external liquidity providers, reduce information leakage, and lower trading costs"; "$95T+ Notional traded per year"; "electronic credit" listed as an emerging Systematic strength.
  - INFERRED: BlackRock is crossing more flow internally. That cuts both ways for venues: less flow sent to dealers, but more electronic and algorithmic trading through whatever goes external.
- **Aladdin as a connectivity layer:**
  - 2025: "51 million trade allocations per year through BlackRock’s Aladdin platform". Since the 2023 Investor Day, trade allocations are up 1.3x and **API calls 17x**.
  - The Aladdin workflow slide lists "Front office / Trading execution / … / Trade confirmations / Accounting / Back office".
  - "Aladdin Provider" for asset servicers; "200+ insurers powered by Aladdin®"; "360,000+ Aladdin® and Preqin users" (2025, versus "130,000+ Aladdin users" in 2023, labeled "~3x since Investor Day 2023").
  - The 2025 TAM is "$20B+": ~$8B public markets tech, ~$8B private markets tech, ~$5B private markets data.
  - Tech-revenue peers named in a footnote: "STT CRD, CWAN, S&P, MORN, MSCI, ENFN, BR, TEMN".
- **ETFs as bond-market plumbing:**
  - 2023: "Modernize the bond market by unlocking client use cases for fixed income ETFs"; "Access: lower trading costs vs. underlying bond trading"; "9 of 10 largest asset managers use iShares fixed income ETFs"; "43 Official institutions, including 21 central banks"; exchange bid-ask spreads "half the industry average".
  - 2025: "8/10 of the most liquid fixed income ETFs are iShares" (Bloomberg, 1Q25 dollar volume); iShares global fixed income market share ~40%; iBonds AUM $42B; iShares Europe fixed income at 50%+ AUM share; "10 of the top 10 largest asset managers have used iShares bond ETFs"; "8 of the top 10 largest insurance companies use bond ETFs".
  - The 2025 ETF engine slide lists "Evolving traditional models of liquidity and financing" and "Modernizing the market with bond ETFs, digital asset ETPs and tokenization".
- **Index and data providers:**
  - Preqin turns BlackRock into a data vendor, "tripling our desktop reach" (200,000+ desktops; 4,000+ clients; ~$240M ARR, 99% recurring; 13x 2024E P/Revenue).
  - Synergy lever: "Indexing and efficient access to private markets", i.e. "Deliver high-quality private markets benchmarks and data".
  - 2025: "Launched Preqin benchmarks within the Aladdin® platform for joint customers"; "Progressing data strategy for deals, companies and indices".

## 9. Shifts over time (2023 to 2025)

- **From "Platform as a Service" to the whole portfolio across public and private markets.** In 2023 private markets meant eFront plus organic funds ($156B in client assets). By 2025, GIP ($183B AUM), Preqin and HPS (pro-forma ~$280B in private credit and GP/LP solutions) are integrated. The headline target moves from "doubling private markets revenue in 5 years" (2023) to "$400B of cumulative fundraising" for 2025–2030 plus "30%+" of revenue from private markets and technology.
- **Aladdin's language:** "language of portfolios" (2023) becomes "language of the whole portfolio" (2025). The addressable market grows from ~$12.5B to $20B+. Tech revenue goes from $1.4B (2022) to $1.6B (2024), with ACV growth of 14% in 1Q25. The "Low to mid–teen long–term growth target" is unchanged. Client retention slips from 98% to 97% (3-year average).
- **ESG and transition language is sharply reduced.**
  - 2023 had a full "Transition Investing" section (Pinner and Tan): $100B+ transition platform, EU and US pledged climate funding, "Sustainable products driving a tectonic shift in capital" (Lord's EMEA trends slide; split across lines). The 2023 iShares lineup includes "iShares Sustainable and Transition ETFs".
  - 2025 has **no transition section**. Jessica Tan moves to Global Product Solutions. "Energy Transition" survives only as a GIP theme and an HPS strategy name. Infrastructure is reframed as the "“Golden Age”" of AI, data centers and power.
  - Risk-factor wording changes. 2023: "climate–related risks" and "highly qualified and diverse professionals". 2024 deal decks: "increasing focus from stakeholders regarding ESG matters". 2025: "environmental and social-related matters", and "diverse" is dropped ("highly qualified professionals").
- **Crypto and tokenization:** from one line, "Tokenization as a new investment wrapper" (2023), to a full digital-assets business in 2025: "Largest crypto asset manager globally ($50B+ AUM)", IBIT, ETHA, BUIDL, Circle, a European Bitcoin ETP. Digital assets are listed as one of the new "$500M+ businesses".
- **AI:** in 2023, AI appears only as NLP/ML in the private equity case study, via an eFront paper ("AI2 : Alternative Investments Meet Artificial Intelligence"). In 2025, AI is everywhere: "ALADDIN COPILOT", "Accelerating with AI", Systematic LLM benchmarks (BlackRock model accuracy 61% versus GPT-4o 55%, using 40-day forward returns on 500 earnings calls), "Tailored AI research agents" "In the lab", AIP, and the UAE AI investing partnership ($30B). Instead: industry $27T by 2030, active ETF AUM expected to grow 4x, fixed income ETF AUM 2x+. iShares AUM is $4.3T, with $431B of 1Q25 LTM NNB and 7% organic base fee growth.

## 10. Surprises / note-worthy

1. **Larry Fink is absent from both Investor Day slide texts.** Small (CFO) opens and closes both events. Combined with acquired-firm CEOs presenting in 2025, this suggests BlackRock is putting its management bench, not the founder, in front of investors.
2. **HPS's CEO presented at Investor Day before the deal closed** ("Pending close"). His section frames a ~$280B combined platform, which signals how far integration had gone ahead of regulatory approval.
3. **Deal multiples rise each time:**
   - GIP: ~25–29x 2024E P/FRE; peers at 25–35x; 15% IRR.
   - Preqin: 13x 2024E P/Revenue; ~18% IRR; dilutive.
   - HPS: ~30x 2025E P/FRE; peers at 30–34x; 16% IRR; 100% stock via SubCo units.

   BlackRock repeatedly justifies the price by the *peer multiple* it expects private markets earnings to command: "durable private markets and technology revenue streams, commanding higher multiples". That is a valuation re-rating play.
4. **Internalization numbers (25% of equity trades offset; ~$600M saved; "reduce information leakage") go further than the 2023 execution-cost framing.** For a Tradeweb study, this is BlackRock signaling it will capture more of the spread economics itself.
5. **BlackRock used Preqin as a source in its own 2023 deck and GIP deck before buying it.** The Preqin deal turns the market's private-markets data referee into a BlackRock subsidiary while BlackRock is a top GP. The deck's promise of "information barriers" shows BlackRock knows this is sensitive.
6. **"$9 billion" returned to LPs since the GIP close sits next to "~40 million US investors using iShares ETFs" among the 2025 foundational stats.** In 2023 the 40 million figure was global; in 2025 it is US-only. Read that metric carefully.
