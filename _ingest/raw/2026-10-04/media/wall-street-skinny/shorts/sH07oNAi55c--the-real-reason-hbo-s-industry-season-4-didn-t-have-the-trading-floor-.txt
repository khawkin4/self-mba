# The Real Reason HBO’s Industry Season 4 Didn’t Have the Trading Floor We All Love…
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sH07oNAi55c | NA | NAs | 2000 views

We have heard from so many of you guys who are sad that Industry season 4 didn't have the trading floor and it's really funny because if you think about why, it really pulls back to like one minor thing from season 3 episode 7, the Pierpoint is Lehman episode. Pierpoint was on the brink of bankruptcy because they apparently had loaded up on too many ESG investments hoping to be book runners on the eventual IPOs. Turns out that all the investments they made were absolute. So, Eric and Adler and other partners are basically trying to save the company and so they go to Mitsubishi who by the way was the actual firm that rescued Morgan Stanley in 2008. I should know, I was there. But anyway, Eric torpedoes the whole thing by pointing out one small mistake on like page 12 of the deck. Now, is that a little bit dramatic? Sure. But the part that isn't is that literally those types of mistakes, those little details, they can actually cost you credibility with a client in investment banking and be the reason that for example your bank doesn't get the huge IPO or the huge deal. This is why bankers are so obsessed with details. And this is why we love Macabacus' deck check. It uses AI to verify that the numbers all tie, right? It scans the entire deck to find mismatched revenue figures, miscalculation, contextual inconsistencies, even across different slides. Maybe had the bankers creating the Pierpoint slide deck used it, there would actually be a trading floor in season 4.
