Should you go to business school? We get asked every single day, so I made a decision tree. First question, are you currently insanely wealthy? You're already a billionaire who never needs to work another day in their life? No? Are you currently working in your dream job? Love everything about it? No? If you needed to get a new job tomorrow, do you have an incredibly well-connected, highly educated, global network of people who would help you get that job? No? Do you want to get a job in a high-paying role like private equity, investment banking, or something else that has super restrictive recruiting cycle? And frankly, whether the answer is yes or no, the last question is, did you already go to business school? If so, you probably don't need to go to business school again.
