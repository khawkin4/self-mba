# Why I walked away from over $200k of my bonus at Morgan Stanley:
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEAVjyCnOLU | NA | NAs | 8600 views

You can be vice president for ten years, right? Some never leave this "vice presidential prison" at all. For such vice presidents, that $500,000 bonus will likely be split one-third, one-third, and one-third. One third in cash, one third in deferred funds that can be invested but to which you do not have access, that is, you invest in assets that cannot be touched, and one third in deferred shares that become available within three to five years. So that's when you suddenly feel like leaving the company is completely unprofitable. And to your point, Kristen, this is why at this point people are saying, "Okay, I'm cheap enough that someone could buy out all of my accumulated deferred payments and it would make financial sense for them." However, if you put in many years of this kind of work, someone will have to pay for you and buy out all those deferred payments, so you become a very expensive hire.
